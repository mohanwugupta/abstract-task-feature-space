## What is new?

All **540 human-generation calls** were already available. Applicability coverage has now increased from **69,365 to 230,271 definitive cells** in the independent **60 × 5,233** human-only matrix. The refresh adds **160,906** definitive cells, with no previously definitive cell changing or becoming unknown. The historical 75-item pooled matrix is not used as a mixed human/task representation.

The normative feature-space comparison is restricted to the same **36 Vinson concepts**, preserving action-noun/action-verb/object identity, original frequencies, and all **1,029 published coordinates**. Their LLM applicability matrix contains **135,414 definitive cells out of 188,388 (71.9%)**. The remaining 28.1% are unknown. Finishing the eligible request queue does not imply a complete binary dataset.

The 24 Harpaintner concepts have aggregate category profiles rather than individual human feature strings. Their category comparison remains unavailable pending reviewed translations and generated-feature coding. No new reliability ceiling or participant LOOCV is claimed.

## Independent generation-based comparison

The earlier primary analysis used the first ten features per call to balance output yield. Its uncapped-output sensitivity already existed. This refresh reruns it and foregrounds **every accepted output**, retaining all three repetitions and counting a canonical feature once per repetition.

| Prompt | Full-output RSA rho | Permutation p | Holm p | Zero off-diagonal similarities |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| P0 | .186 | .0001 | .0003 | 98.3% |
| P1 | .189 | .0002 | .0004 | 95.1% |
| P2 | .114 | .0006 | .0006 | 99.4% |

The balanced first-ten results remain .117/.189/.114. Thus using all P0 outputs improves its observed correspondence, but output budgets differ: the same number of calls produced more P0 propositions. This is not a controlled superiority claim.

These are small positive relational associations. Extreme exact-feature sparsity limits how much conceptual geometry the comparison measures; sparse overlap does not establish that the model lacks semantic structure, and low permutation p-values do not establish broad recovery. Finishing applicability judgments cannot change a representation defined only by generation frequency.

## New generator-plus-judge comparison

Each prompt's coordinate pool contains features independently generated by that family in the human experiment. The Combined pool is their union, with shared columns counted once. Binary applicability judgments replace generation frequency as the LLM weights; the human vectors still use published frequencies. This evaluates a **generator-plus-judge procedure**, distinct from spontaneous generation.

| Pool | Candidate features | Definitive coverage | Complete columns | Fixed-space rho | Conditional pairwise rho |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| P0 | 2,245 | 75.4% | 67 | −.025 | .413 |
| P1 | 1,463 | 80.2% | 243 | −.075 | .232 |
| P2 | 1,660 | 60.2% | 10 | unavailable | .406 |
| Combined | 5,233 | 71.9% | 316 | −.072 | .380 |

**Fixed-space cosine** retains only features definitive for every one of the 36 concepts. It uses one common coordinate space, but its vocabulary is selected by judge coverage. Only 316/5,233 combined columns survive. The Combined correlation is not significant (p=.2577); P0 and P1 are also nonsignificant. Eighteen P2 concept vectors have zero norm, making its full-cohort RSA unavailable. No concepts are removed to obtain a coefficient.

**Conditional pairwise cosine** uses features definitive for both members of each concept pair. Median coordinate counts are 1,499/1,094/763 for P0/P1/P2 and 3,237 Combined. All three correlations are positive (permutation p=.0001/.0003/.0001; Holm p=.0004 for each). Different pairs use different coordinates, so these are exploratory similarities rather than geometry from a fully observed shared space. Missingness is structured and cannot be assumed random.

The difference between these analyses is scientifically consequential: the larger conditional representation shows correspondence, while the small complete-case representation does not. Neither should be selected alone to claim robust validation or rank prompts.

The full sensitivity grid includes excluding grammatical/metalinguistic columns, restricting discovery origins to the Vinson concepts, and within-original-word-type label permutations. Content-only conditional correlations are .410/.384/.403; Combined .408. A separate observation-mask diagnostic has Combined rho=.052, p=.5597. This weak association does not remove the conditional-cosine selection limitation. All diagnostics, denominators, unknowns, and null distributions are exported.

Every RSA uses the same concept order, 630 upper-triangle pairs, average-rank Spearman correlation, and 9,999 concept-label permutations applying the same shuffle on both axes (seed 20261028). Pairs are never treated as independent observations. Holm adjustments cover the declared family groups; sensitivities are labeled exploratory.

The existing automated semantic matcher covered the first-ten-per-call scope. The aligned mean cosines remain **.663/.589/.536** on the same 35 defined concepts; P2/clash remains undefined. These are supervised correspondences with human labels and retain all prior sense, mapping-policy, and grader-calibration caveats.

|---|---:|---:|---:|
| P0 | 2,034 | 1,063 | 971 |
| P1 | 968 | 968 | 0 |

A unit is a canonical feature × target × prompt × repetition, not a distinct vocabulary column. The 971 additional P0 units are **unscored**, not unmatched or invalid. The P0 aligned cosine is therefore not a newly completed full-output score. Extending its semantic matching would require a separately preflighted and authorized inference stage or reviewed annotations; none was launched here.
